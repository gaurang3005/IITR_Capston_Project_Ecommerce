# Loyalty-Points Redemption Policy

Customers earn 1 loyalty point for every Rs.100 of order value that is actually paid and retained.

- One point is worth Rs.1 at checkout, at least 100 points are needed before any redemption, and points may cover no more than 20 percent of a single order's value.
- Points are credited only after the return window closes, so an order that ends as Returned or Refunded earns none, and any points already spent on it are restored to the account.
- Unused points expire 12 months after they are credited, and expired points cannot be reinstated.
- Points cannot be transferred between accounts or converted to cash.
