# Customer-Support Escalation Matrix

Support runs on three tiers, and a ticket starts at the tier that matches the problem rather than always at the first one.

- Level 1 is the chat or phone agent who answers policy questions and raises standard returns, with a first response inside 24 hours.
- Level 2 is a specialist queue for refunds that have missed their promised date, repeat delivery failures, warranty disputes and delayed shipments, and it resolves them within 48 hours.
- Level 3 is a support manager who owns anything still open after Level 2, any claim above Rs.25,000 and any case the customer explicitly asks to escalate, with a 72-hour commitment and a written resolution.
- The order-lookup tool returns an escalation score for every order, and a ticket at or above the documented threshold is routed straight to Level 2 instead of starting at Level 1.
