# Reverse-Pickup Eligibility

Most returns are collected from the customer's address by a reverse-pickup courier at no charge.

- Pickup is available on serviceable pincodes for Apparel, Footwear, Home and Electronics, provided the item is in its original packaging with all tags, manuals and free gifts.
- Beauty products are picked up only when they are unopened and sealed, or when the claim is for damage or a wrong item.
- Where the pincode is not serviceable for pickup, the customer may self-ship to the returns centre and claim the courier cost up to Rs.200 against a receipt.
- Three failed pickup attempts close the request, and a fresh return must then be raised inside the original return window.
