# Damaged-Item Claim Process

A damaged, leaking, expired or wrong item must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, while the courier's liability window is still open.

- The customer uploads photographs of the outer packaging, the shipping label and the item itself, which the claims team reviews within 2 business days.
- An approved claim is settled as a free replacement where stock exists, or as a full refund including any shipping fee, within 3 to 5 business days of approval.
- Beauty is the one category where a seal-broken or leaking unit is accepted back despite the hygiene rule, because the fault is ours.
- A claim raised after 48 hours is assessed case by case and may be declined without photographic evidence.
