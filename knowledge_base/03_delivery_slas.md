# Delivery SLAs

Delivery time is quoted from the moment an order moves to Shipped, not from when it was Placed.

- Metro pincodes are served in 2 to 3 business days, tier-2 and tier-3 towns in 4 to 6 business days, and remote or hill pincodes in 7 to 9 business days.
- An order is flagged as a delayed shipment once it crosses its promised window without a delivery scan.
- Bulky Home items move on a separate appointment-based schedule and can take up to 10 business days.
- Public holidays and regional strikes pause the clock, and the revised date is shown on the tracking page.
