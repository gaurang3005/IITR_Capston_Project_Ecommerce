# Return Window by Product Category

Nykaa accepts returns within a window that depends on what was bought, because resale risk differs by category.

- Apparel and Footwear may be returned within 14 days of delivery, Home items within 10 days, and Electronics within 7 days.
- Beauty products are returnable within 7 days only while the seal, shrink-wrap and batch sticker are intact, since opened cosmetics cannot be resold for hygiene reasons.
- The window is counted from the delivery date recorded on the order, not from the date the order was placed.
- An order already marked Returned or Refunded cannot start a second return.
