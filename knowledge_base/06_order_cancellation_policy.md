# Order-Cancellation Policy

A customer can cancel an order at no cost at any time while its status is still Placed.

- Once the status becomes Shipped the cancel option is withdrawn, because the parcel is already with the courier; the customer may instead refuse delivery at the door or raise a return after it is Delivered.
- A prepaid cancellation is refunded in full to the original instrument within 3 to 5 business days, while a COD cancellation needs no refund because nothing was paid.
- Nykaa may itself cancel an order for a pricing error, a failed address check or stock that turns out to be unavailable, and the customer is told the reason.
- Personalised and made-to-order items cannot be cancelled once production has begun.
