# Warranty Terms by Category

Warranty is separate from the return window and is honoured by the brand rather than by the marketplace.

- Electronics carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the invoice date, and large Home appliances carry 6 months on the motor or heating element.
- Apparel and Footwear have no ongoing warranty, but a manufacturing defect such as a split sole or a failed seam can be claimed within 30 days of delivery.
- Beauty products carry no warranty at all; expiry, leakage and seal-broken issues are handled as damaged-item claims instead.
- A warranty claim needs the invoice number and, for Electronics, the serial number printed on the device.
