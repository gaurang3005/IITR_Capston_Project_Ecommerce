# International Shipping Restrictions

International delivery is offered to a limited set of countries and is always prepaid, because cash on delivery is not available outside India.

- Several Beauty lines cannot travel by air at all: aerosols, nail polish, perfumes and other flammable or pressurised goods are barred by airline dangerous-goods rules.
- Electronics containing lithium batteries ship only within declared watt-hour limits, and large Home appliances are not exported because of voltage and service-network differences.
- The customer is the importer of record and pays any customs duty, local tax and clearance charge on arrival, which Nykaa can neither estimate nor absorb.
- Return windows and warranty do not apply to international orders, so these shipments are final sale apart from damage reported within 48 hours.
