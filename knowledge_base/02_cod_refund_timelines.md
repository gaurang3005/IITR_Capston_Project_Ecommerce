# Cash-on-Delivery Refund Timelines

A cash-on-delivery order is paid in cash at the door, so its refund cannot be reversed to a card and must be sent to a bank account the customer confirms.

- Once the returned parcel reaches our warehouse and passes quality check, the refund is initiated within 2 business days and credited in 5 to 7 business days.
- Prepaid orders are faster, because the money goes back to the original payment instrument in 3 to 5 business days.
- If a COD refund has not arrived 10 business days after the quality check cleared, support raises a payment trace with the bank.
- We never ask for a full card number, a CVV or a net-banking password to process any refund.
